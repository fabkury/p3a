Wi-Fi setup

Connect to Wi-Fi

Device names are useful only if you have more than one p3a. If you have just one, it's more convenient to leave it empty.

This name will become part of the device's LAN host name. Lowercase letters, numbers, and hyphens only.

After saving, p3a will reboot and join your Wi-Fi network. Then open http://p3a.local/ to control your p3a.

Saved! Rebooting now

Connecting to:

p3a has saved your credentials and will now reboot. The p3a-setup network will cease to exist and p3a will connect to the Wi-Fi network you provided.

  • After the device turns on, wait a few seconds for it to connect to Wi-Fi.
  • Then, with your phone or computer on that same Wi-Fi network, open to control your p3a.
  • If p3a cannot connect (a mistyped password, for example), it will offer the p3a-setup network again for you to reconfigure.
