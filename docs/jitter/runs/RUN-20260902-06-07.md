## The two stalls (RUN-06, 16:51:23, seq 186592/186593)

One event, two consecutive frames of one artwork: Makapix post 3621
('m o n s t e r' channel, gen 170, 60–70 ms frames, produce median 72.5 ms,
i.e. a producer-bound artwork with no margin at all). Decode jumped from
its ~55 ms norm to 189, 219 and 281 ms on three frames while the upscale
stayed at 16–17 ms; lateness 137, 166 and 232 ms (the first is below the
analyzer's bar after the overrun rule, the other two are in scope). Five
UART stall reports fired on the same artwork between 16:49:20 and 16:51:23,
i.e. every report of the run is this one artwork.

What was happening: the download task (5779a3e4, core 0) was writing 32 KB
chunks that took 35–43 ms each (card busy), and the loader (83b3881a, core 1)
was reading the next artwork in 64 KB reads that queued behind those writes
for 57–144 ms, plus a `sd_write 512` of 59 ms. In the 3 s before the worst
frame: 66 `sd_read 512`, 7 `sd_write 512`, 5 `sd_write 32768`, 29 flash ops.
Not a command storm (the RUN-11 storms of 2026-08-29 were 170–400 commands
per 100 ms); a congested SD bus under a zero-margin decoder.

Is it wrapper-specific? Not decidable from the soaks: post 3621 was picked
8 times in the wrap soak and **0 times in the patch soak** (random channel
selection), and the patch soak's 69 zero-margin generations never met the
same download + loader coincidence. Hence the targeted probe
(`zm_reproducer.py`, RUN-20260902-09/10): the same zero-margin artwork under
the same paced writes on both arms.

## Zero-margin probe (RUN-20260902-09 / 09L, wrap arm)

`zm_reproducer.py` with `noise_360_60ms.gif` (60 ms frames, decode 63.7 ms,
produce 82.8 ms: a chronically producer-bound artwork, Phase 6 re-baseline
active). Under paced aligned 32 KB writes (A), the misaligned bounce storm
(B) and 512 single-sector internal writes (C), decode stayed at
63.5–63.9 ms median / 64–65 ms max in every condition, 0 anomalies,
lateness max 30–34 ms. So one CMD13 per tick (the wrapper's rate, 40 polls
during a 40 ms busy period) does not touch a saturated core-1 decoder at
all. The RUN-06 event needed the loader's 64 KB reads queued behind the
writes; the short-dwell variant (09L) could not provoke reloads on a
single-artwork playset (0 loader loads), so that class is left for a
future probe. It does not go through `sdmmc_wait_for_idle()` (reads never
call it), hence it cannot distinguish the arms.
